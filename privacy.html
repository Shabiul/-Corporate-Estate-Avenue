Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Corporate Estate Avenue (“CEA”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through corporateestateavenue.com, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. It is written to align with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information we collect

  • Buyer and tenant enquiries: your name, mobile number, email address, whether you are looking to purchase or rent, preferred property type and configuration, preferred location, budget, and any requirements or project preferences you type in.
  • Owner and seller submissions: your name, phone number, email address, property details (type, name, location, size, price, description, amenities) and any photos you upload.
  • Technical data: basic information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, used for security, rate limiting and keeping the site working.

We only collect what you choose to submit. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To recommend and arrange suitable properties, tenants or buyers.
  • To list, market and represent your property, where you are an owner or seller.
  • To prevent spam and misuse, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated advertising.

3. Consent

We ask you to tick a consent box before you submit any form. By doing so you agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms & Conditions, and you consent to us processing and contacting you about your submission. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us (see section 8).

4. Who we share it with

We share information only where needed to provide our service:

  • Property owners, buyers and tenants you are matched with, limited to what is needed to arrange a viewing or transaction.
  • Service providers that host and run this website: Vercel (hosting) and Supabase (database and image storage). They process data on our behalf.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

Our site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address. If you use a WhatsApp or phone link, that conversation is governed by that provider’s own terms.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and listings for as long as needed to serve you and for our legitimate business and legal record-keeping, then delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your information earlier.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with access restricted to authorised staff. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, withdraw your consent, or raise a grievance about how it is handled.
