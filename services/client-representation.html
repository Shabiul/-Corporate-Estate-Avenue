Client Representation

Whether you are buying or renting, we represent your interests: searching, comparing, negotiating and checking the paperwork on your behalf.

Send an enquiry WhatsApp Sajjad

How we help

  • Search and site selection

    We search based on your needs: timing, location, market price, space specifications, cost versus prestige and amenities.

  • Market surveys and analysis

    You get information backed by research on current market conditions and trends, schools and community amenities.

  • Negotiations

    We negotiate on your behalf for fair terms, with the priority on you and not on the deal.

  • Legal verification

    We coordinate verification of credentials and records with reputed solicitors before you decide.

  • Buyer and tenant representation

    We stay involved through documentation and agreement so that nothing is missed.

This is for you if…

  • You are buying or renting and want an adviser working for you.
  • You do not have time to visit and screen many properties.
  • You want a second pair of eyes on price, terms and documents.
  • You are making an investment and want an objective view.
